# Production evidence, labels and limits

## Evidence classes

| Label | Means | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VERIFIED PRODUCTION | Dated retained deployment/reachability or operational report supports the stated scope | Current RC is deployed; fresh live attestation today |
| OBSERVED DEFECT | Fault condition reproduced/established in the identified code/fixture | An actual client incident, loss, exploit or duplicate effect occurred |
| IMPLEMENTED | Mechanism exists in reviewed source/state slice | It is enabled or installed in production |
| IMPLEMENTED + TESTED | Named local/mock/native/retained-target tests support the boundary | Whole-system acceptance or production replay permission |
| CANDIDATE | Local/staged correction or release proposal | Deployed fix or release GO |
| UNKNOWN | Evidence needed for this boundary is unavailable | Absence/inactivity/safe retry |
| PUBLIC DEMO | Independently implemented synthetic lab behavior | Production implementation or production metrics |

## Production observations retained in reports

- Main serving identity/traffic was attested through 30 October 2026 and distinguished from editable RC source.
- Saved 28 October reports document natural cron completion, exact tracking/fulfilled warehouse checks, queue completion and an expected quantity/reserve change in one recovery case. Separate routine completion and bounded unique CRM samples support ordinary processing.
- Effective bundle map had 96 rules in retained evidence; the independent snapshot had a recorded complete catalog observation.

**Saved reports are not raw private production receipts.** Raw provider/stock/deployment receipts outside the permitted worktree were not reopened or published. There is no claim of a new live audit, lifetime order count, throughput, latency, uptime or uninterrupted operation since the earliest historical record.

## Technical evidence and material limits

- Current full RC differs from production and is not interchangeable with the established controller/settings authority. The later minimal artifact avoids that particular clash but remains NO-GO for authentic-protocol tests, matching publisher and fresh operational proof.
- The production-candidate aggregate runner remains **EXIT1** at the known HTTP409 mutation-sent/manual-review contract; unchanged baseline also reproduces failure. Passing component suites are not an all-green release gate.
- Installed Yellow client version/launcher/resources/pending state is **UNKNOWN**. Reviewed seven-script source and local tests do not establish installed customer bytes.
- Generic automatic resolution of every sent/unknown CREATE/PUT is not implemented. Historical linked stock/request evidence remains incomplete for some held cases.
- Native SQLite/continuity tests do not establish full namespace export or catastrophic restore. Local pacing is not tenant-global rate admission. STOP cannot cancel preaccepted provider work.
- The internal architecture closure retains 21 open evidence records. They are not all reproduced here; any that materially affect a diagram are labeled at that boundary.
